Why This Helps: Reducing Dispute & Increasing Transparency

How objective AI-assisted inspection resolves mandi procurement inconsistencies

Standardized Visual Rules

Applies the exact same visual defect and sizing criteria to every lot, eliminating manual grader variance across centers.

Transparent Segregation

Separates sound Grade A produce from URS and defective bulbs with individual bounding boxes and verifiable evidence.

Recorded Evidence for Disputes

Provides an instant digital quality certificate that both farmers and procurement officers can reference to resolve pricing disagreements.

Consistent Screening

Offers immediate intake screening before manual cutting or secondary verification, speeding up procurement lines while protecting fair value.
